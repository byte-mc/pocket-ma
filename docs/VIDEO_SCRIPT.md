# Pocket MA — Demo Video Script

**Target length:** ~3 minutes
**Read pace:** ~120 words per minute (slow, natural)
**Format:** Voiceover narration. Record one take per act for easier syncing.

---

## ACT 1 — The Hiker (~45 seconds)

> I love being out here. No notifications. No noise. Just the trail.
>
> But out here, there's also no signal. No doctor. And anything can happen.
>
> My ankle was swelling three miles from the trailhead. I wasn't sure if it was a bug bite, poison oak, or something worse.
>
> I could have googled it — if I had signal.
>
> That moment made me think: what if your phone could help you, even out here, even offline?

---

## ACT 2 — The World (~55 seconds)

> But honestly — I'm one of the lucky ones.
>
> I have a good phone. I know how to use it. And when I get back to the car, I have a hospital thirty minutes away.
>
> 1.8 billion people don't.
>
> Community health workers in remote villages. Families hours from the nearest clinic. People who face dengue, malaria, snakebite — with nothing but their own judgment and whatever they can find nearby.
>
> They need triage guidance more than anyone. And they're the ones the internet has left behind.

---

## ACT 3 — The Demo (~60 seconds)

*Spare with words here — let the app speak. Narrate around the demo actions.*

> So here's what we built.

**Recording: Airplane mode clip**
*(notification shade pulled down, airplane mode ON)*

> Gemma 4 is running entirely on this phone. No cloud. No API. No waiting on a server.

**Recording 1: Hiker scenario — Mountain / Wilderness region, English**
*(set location → type symptom → model asks follow-up → answer → triage card appears)*

> Describe your symptoms. It asks a follow-up question — just like a triage nurse would. And there's the assessment. Severity. Likely cause. What to do right now. When to get help.

**Recording 1.5: Image input — new session**
*(tap camera icon → take photo of hand/arm → model responds)*

> It can even read a photo. Show it a wound, a rash, a bite — and it responds.

**Recording 2: Location switch — Southeast China region, Chinese**
*(new session → set location → type same symptom in Chinese → triage card in Chinese)*

> Switch the region. Switch the language. Same model. Same phone. Entirely different clinical picture.

---

## CLOSE (~25 seconds)

> Pocket MA works in English, in Chinese, and wherever Gemma 4 speaks your language.
>
> It runs on a $500 phone. It works in a forest, on a mountain, in a village with no data plan.
>
> Because where you are — or where you were born — shouldn't determine whether you survive.
